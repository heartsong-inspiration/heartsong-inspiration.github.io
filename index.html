Why Heartsong began

Heartsong Inspiration began with one baby doll.

When Soretha Eldridge was a very young girl, maybe 4 or 5 years old, her big sister Theresa had a beautiful Black baby doll with a soft body. Soretha loved that baby doll so much that it never left her heart.

Years later, that memory became Heartsong. Soretha began creating her own dolls: brown-skinned girls and boys with curls, braids, bows and bright eyes. They chase fireflies, hold lanterns in the dark and look up at the stars. Every one of them carries a daily affirmation, the words we wish every heart could hear each morning: you are worthy, you are wanted, and your song matters.

Heartsong is a family endeavor, rooted in Cleveland, Ohio, and in a lifetime of rising above hard places. Soretha's sister Theresa and their baby brother, Sylvester Gene, are part of the Heartsong family as dolls of their own, and Sylvester's memory lives on there.

Today Soretha is working toward the dream that started it all: soft-bodied Heartsong baby dolls you can hold, so every little girl and boy can have a doll that looks like them.

With love, Soretha
