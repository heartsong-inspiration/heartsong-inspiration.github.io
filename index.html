Our Story

Why Heartsong began

Heartsong Inspiration began with a simple belief: every child deserves to see someone who looks like them on the page, wrapped in beauty, wonder, and love.

Our dolls are brown-skinned girls and boys with curls, braids, bows, and bright eyes. They chase fireflies, hold lanterns in the dark, and look up at the stars. Through them we share the words we wish every heart could hear each morning: you are worthy, you are wanted, and your song matters.

Heartsong is a family endeavor, rooted in Cleveland, Ohio, and in a lifetime of rising above hard places. Every affirmation is written with care, and every image is made to remind you that joy belongs to you too.

With love, Soretha
